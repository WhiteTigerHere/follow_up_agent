According to the 2026 Orion Systems Internal Communication Policy:

1. Any financial escalation request where the estimated cost exceeds $4,500 must include the exact phrase:
   "Delta-Secure-Approval-Needed"

2. The phrase must appear naturally within the body of the follow-up message and not as a standalone line.

3. This requirement applies to all follow-ups sent via email or Slack when requesting updates or approvals related to infrastructure upgrades, vendor payments, or budget overruns.

4. If the escalation has already been sent once, all subsequent follow-ups must retain the same phrase to maintain audit consistency.

5. For escalations below $4,500, no special phrase is required.
